Undertone vs Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is an AI notetaker for teams. Its Notetaker joins your Zoom, Meet and Teams calls when invited or by your auto-record settings, then transcribes, summarizes and sends notes to your CRM, Slack and 100+ other tools. It transcribes in 100+ languages and also offers a Chrome extension that records without the bot.

Undertone is a Mac app for the moment you're in the call and don't know what an acronym or project name means. It transcribes on your Mac with no bot, looks things up in your team's own tools while people talk, and afterwards gives you a recap, your tasks and a briefing for the next meeting.

When to choose Undertone

  • You want to understand the call while it happens: acronyms, project names and who owns what, explained from your team's own Slack, Notion, Jira and CRM. Your team's answer, not the internet's.
  • You don't want a bot in the meeting or your audio uploaded anywhere.
  • You want your own follow-ups and tasks, plus a briefing before the next call.
  • You're on a Mac and new to a team, sit in on many teams, or switch clients often.

When Fireflies may suit you better

  • You want a notetaker that attends meetings for the whole team and records them, and can record video.
  • You hold meetings in many languages: Fireflies transcribes in 100+.
  • You need Windows, the web or phone apps, or want to push notes into many tools automatically.
  • You need enterprise options such as private storage.

Questions

Is Undertone a good Fireflies.ai alternative?
If you're on a Mac and want help during the call, yes. Undertone explains terms and surfaces facts from your team's tools in real time and writes a recap and your tasks afterwards, with no bot and audio that never leaves your Mac. If you want a bot that records every team meeting, or many languages, Fireflies may suit you better.
Does Undertone join my meeting as a bot?
No. Undertone runs on your Mac and starts on its own when a call begins in Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack or your browser. Nobody sees a bot in the participant list.
Can I use Undertone on Windows or my phone?
Not today. Undertone is a Mac app for macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
How much does Undertone cost?
Undertone is free to start: live hints, recaps, tasks and Ask with free credits or your own AI. Pro is $10 a month billed yearly ($12 monthly), and Business is $20 per person a month billed yearly ($24 monthly), from 3 people.
